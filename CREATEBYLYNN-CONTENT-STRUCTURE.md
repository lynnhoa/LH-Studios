# createbylynn — Content Structure

@createbylynn isn't an ongoing life like @lynnhoa. Everything points to one roof.

## Identity

**Making beauty look wanted.** "I make beauty products look wanted, and brands book me to do it."
Every post typed Product video, Problem → solution, Beauty editorial or Brand post goes to Creator › Portfolio, the proof for UGC outreach (always paid).

## Base · your voice, posted all the time

A standalone video, or one line on a look or product clip · one picture + one voice.

| Arc | Earns | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Relatable | shares | me… · overdoer · getting-ready POVs |
| Opinion & thoughts | comments | hot takes · random thoughts |
| Sign | a nudge | your sign to… |
| Mantra | mood, identity | mantras · manifesting… |

## Main areas

**Looks · growth: what people come for**

| Arc | Earns | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Makeup looks · Series | the core | named looks per season |
| Hairstyles · Series | your strongest | one hairstyle per video |

**Products · trust: why they believe you**

Product run · Package: one product, 2–3 weeks · pick 2–4 · order stays.

| Arc | Earns | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Receiving | curiosity | unboxing · PR reveal · first reaction |
| 2 Try-on | interest | swatches · try-on list · “the perfect ___” |
| 3 Problem → solution | trust | want → so-i voiceover · before / after |
| 4 Verdict | trust | verdict · worth it? · vs dupe |
| 5 In rotation | it stays | a look or GRWM with it · “still using it” |
| Roundups (outside the run) | several products | lists · favourites · empties |

**Editorial · your own shoots** (one arc, Own Shoots)

## Where does it go?

| You have … | It goes to |
|---|---|
| a thought, hot take, mantra, “me…” moment | Base |
| a makeup look or a hairstyle | Looks |
| one product you'll keep posting | Product run |
| several products: haul, favourites, empties | Roundups |
| an opinion on beauty in general | Base › Opinion & thoughts |
| an opinion on the product you're running | Verdict |
| a product in a look, while its run is live | In rotation |
| your own shoot | Editorial |

---

In the app: Planner › @createbylynn concept: Guide · Arcs on top, Base · Looks · Products · Editorial in the bottom bar (`planner.ts`: `CBL_TABS`, `CBL_RUN`, `CBL_WHO`, `CBL_WHERE`). The arcs come from the data (`LH-Studio-createbylynn-pillars.sql`, then `LH-Studio-createbylynn-tabs.sql`).

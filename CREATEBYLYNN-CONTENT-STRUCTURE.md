# createbylynn — Content Structure

@createbylynn isn't an ongoing life like @lynnhoa. Everything points to one roof.

## Identity (09.11.2026)

**My face, my rules.** One signature face, kept alive with small changes and the products that earn their place on it.

- Name people give me: the girl who always overlines · the girl with the signature face
- Means: I don't chase looks, I have mine: overlined lips, blush, wet lashes, dark chocolate hair
- Personality: particular, loyal, a little obsessed (the same person as @lynnhoa, another object)
- They see (after 5 posts): "she has her face and she knows exactly what goes on it"
- Brand: making beauty look wanted — "I make beauty products look wanted, and brands book me to do it."
- Rule: your face is the constant, products come and go
- Line to @lynnhoa: lynnhoa owns nails, lashes appointments, scent, outfits, trips and occasions; here it's the face and the hair, a trip is only the mirror behind you

Every post typed Product video, Problem → solution, Beauty editorial or Brand post goes to Creator › Portfolio, the proof for UGC outreach (always paid).

## My 3 levels

Three levels, each home or away (the subject travels, the place is only the backdrop), each a storyline of 3–5 posts: Intro (1) → Climax (1) → Outro (1–3). Every post stands alone.

| Level | Intro | Climax | Outro | Home | Away |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Signature · the always (Looks) | the always line, the thought · Presence + Text, me… | the move in the mirror: overline, blush, wet lashes, the bun · Look clip | the result face, the feeling · Selfie Reaction, Mantra, Trend | your moves in your mirror | the same moves in the hotel mirror |
| 2. Change · the storyline (Looks, package) | the want or the thought · Presence + Text, Transition | trying it, trusting the process, the switch · Look clip, want → so-I Voiceover | giving in or the verdict, then living with it · Selfie Reaction + Result, me…, Mantra | a change you live through | a change you can only make there |
| 3. Product run · trust (Products, package) | receiving, first reaction · Unboxing, PR reveal | try-on, problem → solution · Try-on list, want → so-I Voiceover, before / after | verdict, in rotation · Verdict, worth it?, a look with it | what lands at home | what you find there |

Signature = lynnhoa's Curation (the base), Product run = lynnhoa's Upgrade (when something lands), Change = lynnhoa's Project (Rei). Change runs one cycle per step; the Transition carries the reveal.

## Daily (the Base tab, renamed 09.11.2026) · single clips between storylines

A standalone video, or one line on a look or product clip · one picture + one voice. Beauty-specific mantras, "me…" lines, your sign to…, hot takes.

| Arc | Earns | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Relatable | shares | me… · overdoer · getting-ready POVs |

| Sign | a nudge | your sign to… |

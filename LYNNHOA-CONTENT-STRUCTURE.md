# LYNNHOA — Master Content Structure

The structure has separate layers: your identity, the environments you create content in, your personal eras, and the way you package stories.

## 1. Overall identity

**LYNNHOA = Fashion Personality**

The overall personality and world people follow: fashion, beauty, personal image, experiences, opinions and everyday life.

**What I stand for: the art of being put together.**

- Line (bio + the hook of every How I …): "high maintenance so I can be low maintenance"
- Name people give me: the put-together one
- Means: I think before, so the moment needs none
- Personality: particular, a little chaotic, intentional in the end
- They see (after 5 posts): "she's thought of everything, so she's always ready"
- Rule: show the curation, not the chores

## 1b. My 3 levels (09.11.2026)

Three levels, each home or away, each a storyline of 3–5 posts: Intro (1) → Climax (1) → Outro (1–3). Every post stands alone. High maintenance = the Before (Curation, Project), low maintenance = the Moment (Upgrade, Daily clips).

| Level | Intro | Climax | Outro |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Curation · the Before | a thought, an idea, the urge · Presence + Text | How I …: the hook → the edit → the result · Seated / Half-Body Talking | the feeling after, or in use if ready to use · Selfie Reaction, Trend, Walk + Text |
| 2. Upgrade · the Moment (The Outing) | getting ready, just the outfit · GRWM Talking | the look · Outfit Check | the moment, 1–3 trends at the place · TikTok Trends, Lip Sync Song, Selfie Reaction, Vlog optional |
| 3. Project · the long Before | a transition or a thought · Transition, Presence + Text | the progress · Vlog, Selfie Reaction + Result | the styling or the reaction · Outfit Check, Walk + Text, Selfie Reaction, Trend |

Curation at home = the prep, the edit; away = what I shop to bring home and why, my taste. Upgrade and Project are the same home or away. A Project runs one cycle per step; the Transition carries the reveal.

## 4. Signature Series: How I …

The title is **"How I …"**, its ending changes with the topic. Right below it, the hook: the real reason you started to curate. The video shows your curation and the result, not the chores.

Pattern: **Hook (why I started) → Curation → Result**. Filmed as **The Edit** (Seated or Half-Body Talking). It runs only in these Home chapters:

| Chapter | Title | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | How I stay ready for anything | "I'm high maintenance so I can be low maintenance" |
| Home Projects | How I set up my bathroom | "My bathroom made my mornings complicated" |
| Wardrobe & Style | How I edit my wardrobe | "I had no space for new clothes" |
| Beauty Projects | How I build my matching makeup sets | "My drawer was messy and random, I had no favourites, and I stopped enjoying makeup" |
| Kits & Preparation | How I build my travel kit | "I used to pack at 4 AM" |

These are the starting episodes; the titles and hooks are changed in the app (Guide › How I … › edit, saved per chapter, no history).

## 8. Context, Format & Setup

Supporting production layers, not additional content categories.

| Term | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Context | What you're doing within a phase | Occasion, preparation, organisation, maintenance, seasonal change, shoot |
| Format | How the content is presented | Talking, outfit video, vlog, voiceover, editorial |
| Setup | Where/how you film | Talking Spot, Mirror Spot, project location |

Your Talking Spot can be the recognisable home base for How I ….

## The complete hierarchy

- **LYNNHOA — Fashion Personality**
  - **Personal Eras:** Beauty · Style · Signature
  - **Main Areas:** Home · Travel · Brand Work
    - **Phases:** what kind of activity or experience is happening?
  - **Content Types**
    - Daily (single clips)
    - Signature Series: How I … (in The Edit)
    - Content Package: The Outing (Before → Look → Moment)
    - Standalone Content Packages
      - Arcs → Posts (narrative approach → individual content pieces)
      - Context · Format · Setup (how the content is situated and produced)

**The key distinction:** Personal Eras describe the image you're developing. Main Areas and Phases describe what's happening in your life. Series and Packages turn those things into stories. Base Posts keep people connected to you in between.
